Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

What the game collects

Nothing. Colonial Skies does not collect, store or share any personal information.

The game keeps your campaign progress, settings and the battle in progress on your device only. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

Purchases

The only time the game talks to the internet is when it asks the App Store which items you've bought, or when you make a purchase. Apple handles every purchase; the game never sees your payment details or your Apple Account.

This website

These pages have no cookies, analytics or trackers. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and questions

If the game ever changes what it keeps, this page will change with it and show a new date. Questions are welcome on the support page.
